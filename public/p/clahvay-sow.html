Your Tech Stack

Every tool chosen for a reason. Fast, affordable, and built to scale with you.

  • Cloudflare Pages
    Frontend hosting. Flat-rate pricing, global CDN, unlimited bandwidth. Your site loads fast everywhere, costs stay predictable.
  • Convex
    Backend and database. Handles student data, bookings, video progress, content management. Real-time updates, built-in security.
  • Square
    Bookings, payments, and CRM. Your existing Square Premium account — everything stays in your dashboard. No new tools to learn.
  • Bunny.net
    Video hosting and streaming. No ads, your branding, DRM protection. Affordable at any scale — pennies per GB.
  • Ecwid
    E-commerce for merch. Product catalog, cart, and checkout embedded directly on your site.
