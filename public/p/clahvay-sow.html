Your Tech Stack

Built on the stack you said aligns with your needs. Cloudflare stays as a backup option if we ever need to migrate.

  • Vercel
    Frontend hosting. Generous free tier, global CDN, instant deployments, zero-config Next.js. Used by Hulu, Nike, TikTok. Cloudflare Pages is a 1-day migration if we ever need to switch.
  • Supabase
    PostgreSQL database, auth, and storage. Open-source, runs on a standard you can take with you anywhere — no vendor lock-in. Generous free tier covers your scale.
  • Square
    Bookings, payments, and CRM. Your existing Square Premium account stays the source of truth — we pull data from it, we don't replace it.
  • Bunny.net (Phase 2)
    Video hosting and streaming. No ads, your branding, DRM available. Affordable at any scale — pennies per GB.
  • Ecwid
    E-commerce for merch. Product catalog, cart, and checkout embedded directly on your site. Replaces BigCommerce.
