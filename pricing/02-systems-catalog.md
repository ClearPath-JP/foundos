# 02 — Systems Catalog (customer-facing copy)

This is the copy for system cards (`/systems`) and system pages (`/systems/[slug]`). Prices are NOT written here. They are pulled from `pricing.config.ts`.

**Writing rules**
- Lead with the result for the owner, not the technology. "Take orders online," not "Stripe-integrated checkout."
- Plain words an owner would say out loud. No "leverage," "seamless," "solutions."
- "How it works" is a real sequence, so it gets numbered steps. Nothing else does.
- Never promise rankings, revenue numbers, or review counts.

Each system page uses this structure: **headline → how it works (3 steps) → good fit if → what we need from you → FAQ → price + add to build.**

---

## Website (base, included in every build)

**Headline:** A site that shows your food and gets people to your door.

**What you get:** Your menu with real photos, hours, location, and a tap-to-call button. It's fast on phones, where your customers actually look you up. We host it, keep it secure, and make small updates for you.

**What we need from you:** Your menu, logo (a photo of your sign works), and photos of your food. If you don't have photos, we can take them.

**FAQ**
- *Do I own it?* DECIDE (depends on the cancellation policy).
- *Can I change my prices later?* Yes. Text or email the change and we update it.

## Truck Tracker

**Headline:** Customers always know where your truck is.

**How it works**
1. You text your spot, like "tomorrow 11 to 3 at Marietta Square" or "closed today."
2. Your site updates itself and texts you back to confirm.
3. Customers see whether you're open now, where you'll be next, and get directions in one tap.

**Good fit if:** you move around, and customers ask "where are you today?" in your comments or reviews.

**What we need from you:** The phone number you'll text from, and your usual spots.

**FAQ**
- *What if I type the location wrong?* You get a confirmation text. Reply FIX to change it. If a location is unclear, it asks you instead of guessing.
- *Do I need an app?* No. Just regular texting.

## Online Ordering

**Headline:** Take pickup orders online without paying app commissions.

**How it works**
1. Customers pick their food on your site and pay by card.
2. You get the order by text, on a tablet, or on a printer.
3. They pick it up. No line, no phone call, no 15–30% delivery app cut.

**Good fit if:** your line gets long, your phone rings during rush, or you're tired of giving delivery apps a cut of every order.

**What we need from you:** Your full menu with prices, and whether you want orders by text, tablet, or printer.

**FAQ**
- *Are there card fees?* Yes, standard card processing fees apply, the same as any card reader.
- *Can I stop orders when I'm slammed?* Yes. Text PAUSE to stop and RESUME to start again. Text "SOLD OUT" plus an item name to hide it.
- *Does it work for my truck?* Yes. Ordering only opens while your truck is marked open.

## Catering Leads

**Headline:** Turn catering requests into booked jobs.

**How it works**
1. Customers fill out a short form: date, headcount, location, and what they want.
2. They get an instant reply, and you get every detail in one text.
3. If they don't book, a friendly follow-up goes out for you.

**Good fit if:** you cater, and requests get lost in DMs, voicemails, or emails you don't get to in time.

**What we need from you:** Your catering menu or price ranges, and minimum order size.

**FAQ**
- *Do I still talk to the customer?* Yes. This makes sure you hear about every request fast, and nobody goes cold before you reply.

## Missed Call Text-Back

**Headline:** Every call you miss gets a text back.

**How it works**
1. You miss a call because you're busy.
2. The caller instantly gets a text with your menu and ordering link.
3. If they reply, you see the conversation and can answer when you're free.

**Good fit if:** your phone rings during rush and nobody can pick up.

**What we need from you:** Your business phone number.

**FAQ**
- *Do I need a new phone number?* DECIDE based on the build (call forwarding vs. new number).

## Review Booster

**Headline:** Get more Google reviews without asking in person.

**How it works**
1. A customer orders.
2. A little later, they get a short text asking how it was, with a link to review you on Google.
3. More reviews help new customers trust you and help you show up on Google.

**Good fit if:** people love your food, but only a few ever leave reviews.

**What we need from you:** Your Google Business Profile link.

**FAQ**
- *Can it send only happy customers to Google?* No. Google doesn't allow that, and it can get your reviews removed. Every customer gets the same link. They can also send you private feedback if they'd prefer.

## Google Boost

**Headline:** Show up higher on Google when people search for food near them.

**What we do:** Set up and clean up your Google Business Profile, keep your hours and menu correct everywhere, post updates and new photos regularly, and send you a simple monthly report of calls and direction requests.

**Good fit if:** you have a fixed location and people nearby aren't finding you.

**What we need from you:** Access to your Google Business Profile (we'll help you set it up if you don't have one).

**FAQ**
- *How fast does it work?* It usually takes a few months to see a difference. Anyone promising #1 on Google is guessing.

## Text Club

**Headline:** Bring customers back with a text.

**How it works**
1. Customers join by texting a word to your number or checking a box when they order.
2. You send specials, new menu items, or where the truck is today.
3. Regulars come back more often. They can leave anytime by replying STOP.

**Good fit if:** you have regulars and want them coming back more often.

**What we need from you:** What you'd want to send and how often (we recommend no more than once or twice a week).

**FAQ**
- *Can I text my existing customer list?* Only people who agreed to get texts from you. Texting people who didn't sign up breaks carrier rules and can get your number blocked.

## AI Receptionist

**Headline:** Your phone gets answered 24/7.

**How it works**
1. A customer calls.
2. The AI assistant answers, introduces itself as your virtual assistant, and handles common questions like hours, location, and menu.
3. It can text the caller your menu or ordering link while they're on the phone, and passes anything it can't handle to you.

**Good fit if:** your staff spends rush hour answering the same questions on the phone.

**What we need from you:** Your hours, menu, common questions, and when to transfer calls to a person.

**FAQ**
- *Will callers know it's AI?* Yes. It introduces itself as your restaurant's virtual assistant.
- *Is there a call limit?* Monthly minutes included: DECIDE.
